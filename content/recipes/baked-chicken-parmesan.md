## Ingredients

- 4 thinly sliced chicken breasts, fat trimmed
- 3/4 cup seasoned Italian bread crumbs
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1 Tbsp garlic powder
- 1 Tbsp italian seasoning
- 2 Tbsp butter, melted
- 1 egg
- 1 mozzarella ball
- Marinara sauce

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 450°. Spray parchment paper with non-stick spray on baking sheet(s). Wash and pat dry chicken, and make sure it's pounded thin.
2. Combine parmesan cheese, bread crumbs, garlic powder, and italian seasoning in a medium bowl.
3. Add egg to melted butter in a small bowl. Brush chicken with mixture, then cover chicken with breadcrumb mixture. Place on baking sheet. Lightly spray with non-stick spray on top.
4. Bake 25 minutes. Turn chicken over, bake another 10 minutes or until crispy.
5. Remove from oven, spoon a dollop of marinara sauce on each piece of chicken, and top with 1 1/2 Tbsp of mozzarella cheese. Bake another 5 minutes.
6. Serve on top of cooked spaghetti or with asparagus.
