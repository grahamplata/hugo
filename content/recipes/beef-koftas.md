## Ingredients

### Beef Koftas

- 1 pound ground meat (80% beef, 20% lamb)
- 1/2 red onion, chopped
- 2 Tbsp dill (fresh really makes a difference)
- 2 Tbsp parsley (fresh really makes a difference)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1.5 Tbsp paprika
- 1.5 Tbsp oregano
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp salt
- Pepper to taste
- Pita flatbreads (2-4)
- Tzatziki sauce
- Optional feta cheese crumbles
- Optional french fries as a side

### Salad

- 1 cucumber, peeled and diced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1/4 red onion, diced
- 1.5 tsp sumac
- 1 Tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Salt

## Directions

1. Optional: Preheat oven for french fries. Cook fries per package instructions.
2. Mix all kofta ingredients in a large bowl. Form into small logs or balls, set aside. Pan fry or grill until golden and cooked through.
3. Toss salad ingredients in a bowl to combine. Assemble the pita (pro tip: pre-warm them in the microwave for about 30 seconds!) with the tzatziki sauce, add kofta & salad.
4. Finish with optional feta cheese and fresh dill. Serve fries on the side.
